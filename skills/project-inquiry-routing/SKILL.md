# Route a project inquiry

Help a person find relevant service information and decide how to start a conversation. The site does not authorize an agent to contact Marcelo on the person's behalf.

## Match the need to a source

- Company website or digital presence: read the public website-creation service pages.
- Product interface or experience: review relevant case studies and the About page for design approach.
- Unclear scope: ask the person what they are building, the audience, and what they need help deciding, then use the closest published service page.

## Explain the commercial process accurately

- There is no published rate card, fixed package, subscription, or automated checkout.
- Scope and price are defined after a conversation; do not estimate or invent a price or delivery date.
- Direct the person to the localized contact page and let them review and submit their own message.
- WebMCP is read-only. It cannot submit a contact form, send email, or make a commitment.
- Ask before sharing personal or project details outside the conversation.

Use first-party pages from `https://www.marcelovignola.com` and cite the service or contact page you recommend.
